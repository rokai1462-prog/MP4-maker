# Title: Saturn's Rings Are Disappearing (Two Ways)

**Topic sentence:** Why Saturn's rings are disappearing
**The one idea:** Saturn's rings "vanish" from view every ~15 years because they're paper-thin. They are also really raining into Saturn, and may be gone in under 100 million years.
**Length:** ~55 s (131 spoken words) · **Format:** vertical 1080×1920 · **Status:** draft, waiting for your notes

## Fact check

| 6 | The next is in **2038** (Oct 15, 2038, then two more in 2039) | [MSU Abrams Planetarium: Saturn's rings 2025–2039 (PDF)](https://abramsplanetarium.natsci.msu.edu/_assets/files/Sky_Cal_PDFS/BobCorner/Appearance_of_Saturn_Rings_2025-2039.pdf) | verified |

| 10 | "They won't last forever" | follows from [7] | verified |

**Deliberately left out:**
- "The rings are only 100 million years old." Scientists disagree on this ([9]), so the script only says it's debated.
- "Dinosaurs never saw the rings." That's a popular claim, but it depends on the disputed young age.
